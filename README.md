# 🎴 PGCC = TCG Sourcing & Market Simulator

A web-based trading card game simulation where you can buy sealed product, rip open 10-card booster packs with realistic stack-sliding mechanics, collect rare pulls, and submit raw cards to PSA, CGC, or Beckett for grading.

## 🚀 Live Demo
Play the game live here: https://your-username.github.io/your-repo-name/

## ✨ Features
- **Realistic Pack Opening:** Rip packs and reveal 10 facedown cards stacked on top of each other.
- **Grading Company Selection:** Choose between PSA, CGC, and Beckett with tiered valuation fees.
- **Dynamic Marketplace:** Sourcing shop, portfolio net worth tracking, and market resale engine.
